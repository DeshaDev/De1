# Home page override

Follow `design-system/baseera/MASTER.md`.

- Keep the hero welcoming, Arabic-first and calm, with one prominent “هيا نبدأ” action and a subordinate accessible-reader action.
- Preserve the title “محاكاة جلسة القراءة التفاعلية”. The preview should highlight words and speak the selected word. It must not display gaze traces, eye fixation timings, calibration, risk scores or claim to run a live assessment.
- Place four clearly labeled activity cards below the preview: reading, numbers/math, accessible reader, and practice.
- Explain the learner journey in three short steps. Keep parent/teacher reporting visually separate from child activities.
- State that results are educational and non-diagnostic in respectful language.
- Use consistent SVG/Lucide artwork; decoration must not reduce contrast or distract from the reading line.
- On mobile, stack the hero, preview and activity cards. Avoid horizontal scrolling; provide pause controls and respect reduced motion.
